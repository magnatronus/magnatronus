Now Departing

Now Departing is a mobile app for both Android and iOS (iPhone) that communicates with the UK Live Departure Board Web Service (LDBWS) provided by National Rail Enquiries to look up specific departures from a selected station.

This is the same information that is displayed on the departure boards at various UK railway stations, but now you can access it from your mobile device.

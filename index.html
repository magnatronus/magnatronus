Now Departing

Now Departing is a mobile app for both Android and iOS (iPhone) that communicates with the UK Live Departure Board Web Service (LDBWS) provided by National Rail Enquiries to look up specific departures from a selected station. This is the same information that is displayed on the departure boards at various UK railway stations.

DaysLeft

DaysLeft is a mobile application for keeping track of your Schengen allowance if you are affected by the 90/180 day rule for visa-free travel. It was originally created to help me track my Schengen visits to make sure I stayed within the 90/180 rule.
